\subsection{What Macroeconomics Studies（宏观经济学研究什么）}\label{what-macroeconomics-studiesux5b8fux89c2ux7ecfux6d4eux5b66ux7814ux7a76ux4ec0ux4e48}

Macroeconomics is the study of the economy as a whole（整体经济）. Topical issues:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Recessions（衰退）: what causes them, and why government stimulus（政府刺激）may help;
\item
  How housing market problems spread to the rest of the economy;
\item
  Government budget deficits（预算赤字）and their effects on workers, consumers, firms, taxpayers;
\item
  Inflation and the rising cost of living（通胀与生活成本）;
\item
  Why many countries are poor and what policies promote growth（增长政策）;
\item
  Trade deficits（贸易赤字）and national well-being.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Economic Models（经济模型）}\label{economic-modelsux7ecfux6d4eux6a21ux578b}

Models are simplified versions of reality（简化现实）:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  irrelevant details are stripped away;
\item
  used to show relationships between variables, explain economic behavior, and devise policy.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Endogenous vs exogenous variables（内生 vs 外生变量）}\label{endogenous-vs-exogenous-variablesux5185ux751f-vs-ux5916ux751fux53d8ux91cf}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Endogenous variables}（内生变量）: values determined inside the model;
\item
  \textbf{Exogenous variables}（外生变量）: determined outside the model, taken as given.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Multiple models（多个模型的必要性）}\label{multiple-modelsux591aux4e2aux6a21ux578bux7684ux5fc5ux8981ux6027}

No single model answers every question. For each new model, keep track of:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  its assumptions（假设）;
\item
  which variables are endogenous / exogenous;
\item
  the questions it can answer and those it cannot（能回答/不能回答的问题）.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prices: Flexible vs Sticky（弹性 vs 黏性价格）}\label{prices-flexible-vs-stickyux5f39ux6027-vs-ux9ecfux6027ux4ef7ux683c}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Market clearing}（市场出清）: the assumption that prices are flexible and adjust to equate supply and demand.
\item
  \textbf{Short run}（短期）: prices are sticky, so demand may not equal supply --- this helps explain unemployment (excess supply of labor) and unsold goods.
\item
  \textbf{Long run}（长期）: prices are flexible, markets clear, and the economy behaves differently.
\end{itemize}
